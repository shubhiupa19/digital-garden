New York. Dallas. Paris. Seattle.

I was fortunate to have the opportunity to live in four cities over the past
four years of my undergraduate career.

The most valuable lessons I took from it are these: 

- It's going to hurt. Let it.
- Open yourself up to new ways of doing things
- Be okay with being alone
- Let people surprise you
